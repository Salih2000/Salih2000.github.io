I'm Salih, a SAS programming and data management specialist based in Istanbul. I work on SAS Viya migrations, data warehouse transformations, and SQL and ETL development for enterprise clients in telecom, energy, insurance, retail and fintech. I combine this work with AI technologies to design and develop original solutions for organizations and individuals.

Data Management Assistant Specialist at [Sade.AI](https://www.sade.ai/).

## SAS programming and SAS Viya migration

I write and tune SAS code with DATA step, PROC SQL, SAS Macro and CASL, and I work in SAS Studio, Enterprise Guide and SAS Visual Analytics. Most of my projects are SAS migrations and modernizations: moving analytical models, reports and flows from SAS 9.4 and SAS Viya 3.5 to SAS Viya 4. I took part in these migrations at Türk Telekom, Enerjisa and Yves Rocher France. After go-live I also handled SAS consulting, maintenance and support for Türk Telekom and AYDEM Enerji.

## Data management and data engineering

I develop and tune SQL and ETL processes on Oracle, Hadoop, Vertica and PostgreSQL, and I work on data quality, data lineage and data governance. At Allianz I convert models and reports from a legacy schema to a Kimball star schema on Oracle. For Türk Telekom I wrote a Python program that analyzed more than 70,000 Oracle and Hadoop tables to find which ones were in use and how the models depended on them.

## AI in data work

I orchestrate AI agents, add AI steps to existing ETL processes, and build custom LLM assistants and chatbots that comply with KVKK and keep company information inside the company.

All of my projects, my full skill list and my SAS certificates are on my [CV](/cv/). I write about this work on the [blog](/blog/).
